Who We Are

Turning experience into opportunity.

The Institute for Research and Retirement (IRR) was established in 2021 to harness the knowledge, experience and professional expertise of retired professionals and researchers for national development.

Our Purpose

IRR connects experienced retirees with young researchers, universities, government institutions, development partners and the private sector.

What We Do

Expertise that creates impact.

IRR creates opportunities for retirees and researchers to continue contributing to Rwanda's development through research, consultancy, mentorship and capacity building.

🔬

Research

Conduct applied and policy-oriented research, socioeconomic studies, baseline studies, feasibility studies and impact assessments.

💼

Consultancy

Provide strategic planning, institutional development, monitoring and evaluation, feasibility studies and technical advisory services.

🤝

Knowledge Transfer

Connect experienced professionals with young researchers and junior academics through mentorship and collaborative learning.

🎓

Capacity Building

Organize training, workshops, seminars and conferences while strengthening research and professional skills.

🌍

Partnerships

Establish partnerships with universities, government, private-sector organizations and development partners.

📊

Evidence-Based Development

Support better policies and programmes through high-quality research, knowledge sharing and professional expertise.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading centre of excellence in research, consultancy and intergenerational knowledge transfer, contributing to sustainable development in Rwanda and beyond.

Our Mission

To mobilize and apply the knowledge, experience and expertise of retirees and researchers through high-quality research, consultancy, capacity building and knowledge-sharing initiatives that support evidence-based decision-making and sustainable development.
